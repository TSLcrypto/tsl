Last updated: September 7, 2026

## 1. Purpose

This document records the outcome of the Blockaid security review concerning the TSL token contract on BNB Smart Chain and provides public references supporting independent verification.

## 3. Review Background

The TSL contract was previously flagged by Blockaid.

The TSL team requested a manual review and provided additional transparency and independently verifiable evidence concerning the token distribution and the primary liquidity pool.

The supporting evidence included:

- Major-wallet allocation documentation
- Primary PancakeSwap V2 TSL/USDT pool identification
- Liquidity-lock evidence
- Public on-chain lock transaction
- Official project and contract references

## 6. Blockaid Review Outcome

Following the submission of additional evidence and further investigation, Blockaid communicated to the TSL team on September 6, 2026 that its investigation concluded that the previous flagging was incorrect and that the flagging had been modified accordingly.
